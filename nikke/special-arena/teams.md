# Special Arena teams

Set on 2026-11-20. Defense CP 3,483,229 · Attack CP 3,400,497.

## Rules that shaped these teams

- Each Nikke can appear once across the three defense teams, and once across the three attack teams. Attack and defense can share units.
- Every team needs at least one B1, one B2 and one B3.
- Scarlet: Black Shadow deals distributed damage. Biscuit's immunity (on a Defender under 50% HP) cuts it to 1 damage, so SBS should face teams without Biscuit.
