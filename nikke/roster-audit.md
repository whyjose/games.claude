3. **Noise has a Max Ammo line (68.93%)** — Prydwen: she must reload to refresh her Max HP buff and self-heal. Change Effects on that piece.
4. **Moran (Treasure) arena cube** — Vigor on Arena, Tempering on Battle. Prydwen's PvP pick is Tempering: swap the Arena slot.
5. **Gear level 0 on Overload pieces** — Grave, Noise, Rumani, Bay, Anis, Rapunzel. Enhance to 5 for stats/CP.
6. **Phase 5 dolls on front-liners** — Blanc (P1) and Noise (P2) want Damage Reduction; level their dolls.
