# Overload rerolling

Checked 2026-11-20 against [Prydwen's reroll guide](https://www.prydwen.gg/nikke/guides/overload-gear-reroll) and [nikke.gg's Overload guide](https://nikke.gg/overload-equipment/).

## Rules

- A piece can't roll the same effect twice. "4× ATK" in a build means one ATK line on each of the 4 pieces.
- Two good lines per piece is the target. Prydwen: fine "in 90% of the situations"; chasing a third is "mostly for competitive players" and "extremely expensive".
- Line chances: line 1 100%, line 2 50%, line 3 30% (nikke.gg).
- A good effect below its minimum counts as half. Reset Attributes until it clears the minimum.

## Minimum values (Prydwen)

| Effect | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Elemental Damage | 19.35% |
| ATK | 9.70% |
| Max Ammo | 52.50% |
| Charge Speed | 4.04% |
| Hit Rate | 9.70% |

Crit Rate, Crit Damage, Charge Damage and DEF have no minimum.

## Custom Module costs

| Locked lines | Reroll | Lock one more |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 2 |
| 1 | 2 | 3 |
| 2 | 3 | 4 |

## Usual flow for a piece

1. Change Effects (no locks, 1 module each) until two target effects are on the piece.
2. If one target line is below its minimum: lock the other one, then Reset Attributes until the low line clears the minimum.
3. Stop and move to the next piece.
